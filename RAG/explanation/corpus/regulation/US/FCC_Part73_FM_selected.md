## US_FCC_FREQ_TOLERANCE — §73.1545(b) FM carrier-frequency departure

- **Regulatory section:** 47 CFR §73.1545(b)(1)–(2)
- **Applicability:** FM stations; the tolerance depends on authorized transmitter output power
- **Status:** Current eCFR
- **Source URL:** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-73/subpart-H/section-73.1545

For an FM station with authorized transmitter output power **more than 10 watts**, departure of the carrier or center frequency may not exceed **±2000 Hz from the assigned frequency**. For an FM station with authorized transmitter output power **10 watts or less**, the departure may not exceed **±3000 Hz from the assigned frequency**.

## US_FCC_FIELD_STRENGTH_CAL — §73.314 field-strength procedure and calibration check

- **Regulatory section:** 47 CFR §73.314(a), (b)(2), and (c)(2)
- **Applicability:** Field-strength measurements formally submitted in rulemaking, measurements requested by the Commission, and community-signal showings under the procedures identified in §73.314
- **Status:** Current eCFR
- **Source URL:** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-73/subpart-B/section-73.314

Section 73.314 directs persons making field-strength measurements for formal submission in rulemaking, or at the Commission's request, to follow its measurement and reporting procedure; it separately identifies the procedure for a showing of signal level over a specific community. Under the propagation-analysis measurement procedure in paragraph (b)(2), **the instrument calibration is checked at each measuring location** before the specified antenna elevation and measurement steps. Under the community procedure in paragraph (c)(2), measurements follow equipment calibration and antenna elevation.

## US_FCC_FIELD_STRENGTH_REPORT — §73.314 calibration and equipment reporting

- **Regulatory section:** 47 CFR §73.314(b)(3)(iv)–(v) and (c)(3)(vi)–(vii)
- **Applicability:** Reports of field-strength measurements made under the corresponding §73.314 propagation-analysis or community-measurement procedure
- **Status:** Current eCFR
- **Source URL:** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-73/subpart-B/section-73.314

The report must identify calibrated measurement equipment and, for each instrument, provide manufacturer, type, serial number, rated accuracy, and date of most recent calibration by the manufacturer or a laboratory. It must also describe the calibration procedure for the measuring equipment, including the field-strength meter, measuring antenna, and connecting cable. Non-standard instruments require complete details.

## US_FCC_MODULATION — §73.1570(b)(2) FM modulation levels

- **Regulatory section:** 47 CFR §73.1570(b)(2)(i)–(ii)
- **Applicability:** FM stations; the subcarrier increase applies only when subsidiary communications services under §73.319 are provided concurrently with stereophonic or monophonic programming
- **Status:** Current eCFR
- **Source URL:** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-73/subpart-H/section-73.1570

FM total modulation must not exceed **100 percent on peaks of frequent recurrence referenced to 75 kHz deviation**. A station meeting the stated subsidiary-communications condition may increase total peak modulation by **0.5 percent for each 1.0 percent of subcarrier injection modulation**, but carrier modulation may never exceed **110 percent (82.5 kHz peak deviation)**.
